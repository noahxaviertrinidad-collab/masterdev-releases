Getting started

Your first 10 minutes

You've got MasterDev open. Here's what you'll see and what we suggest doing, minute by minute.

  1. 0–1 min

    Create your local account

    A @username and a password that lock the window. It only exists on your PC. If you forget it, it can't be recovered, but it can be reset without losing anything (the manual explains how).

  2. 1–4 min

    Watch the intro (or skip it)

    About 3 minutes that walk through everything MasterDev does. You can pause it, skip scenes or close it with Esc, and watch it again anytime with /intro.

  3. 4–6 min

    Pick your path

    💻 Developer

    Your level (beginner to pro), the engines you use and your visual style. MasterDev adapts explanations and templates to them.

    🎮 Player

    Your genres, your platforms and whether you want to playtest. If you also make games, tick it and you get both.

    Both paths see everything; the path only arranges your home screen and suggestions. You can change it later with /setup.

  4. 6–10 min

    Your first real thing

    If you make games

    • In Ideas, jot down a raw idea: one sentence is enough.
    • With Claude connected, ask it to refine the idea and create a project from it: MasterDev makes the design document, the folders and starter code that runs.
    • Without Claude, in Projects you can link the folder of a game you already have, without copying or changing it.
    • If it's a web game, click ▶ Play and try it inside the app.

    If you're here to play

    • In Settings › Appearance, pick one of the 12 themes.
    • In Profile, add your picture, banner and favorite games.
    • Open your welcome packs in Cards.
    • If someone invited you to a MasterDev server, join it in Settings › Network to see friends, games and playtests.

Tip

The command bar

At the bottom there's the MD ❯ bar. Type / to see every command, or /help for the full panel with search.

Optional

Connect Claude

If you have Claude Code, click “Connect Claude” at the top of the app. A console opens so you can sign in with your Claude account once. Without Claude, everything else works.
