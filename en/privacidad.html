Privacy

Your data, plainly

MasterDev is built so your work stays on your PC. This is what leaves it, when, and where it goes.

Last updated: November 6, 2026.

Stays on your PC

Your work and your local account

  • Ideas, projects, tasks, notes, voice notes, library, settings and themes are stored on your PC.
  • The local account (@username and password) only exists on your PC. The password isn't stored: only an encrypted fingerprint.
  • The local account locks the window, but doesn't encrypt your files: anyone using your Windows session can open them.
  • MasterDev has no ads and no third-party trackers.

Only if you join a server

Community

  • The MasterDev server you join stores your public profile, your friends, your messages and what you publish (games, playtests, reviews).
  • Your projects, ideas, tasks, library and settings are never uploaded.
  • People who look you up on the server only see your @username, name, picture and path. Your friends see your full profile.
  • Calls and screen sharing go directly between PCs (P2P), within the same network; they don't go through the server.
  • Playtest telemetry (how a test session is played) is only shared if you accept it for that playtest.
  • You can host your own server: then the data stays on your machine.

Only if you connect Claude

Claude

  • When you give it a task, Claude Code reads and edits that task's files and sends them to Anthropic for processing, using your own Claude account and under its terms.
  • The Community never uses Claude: talking with your friends doesn't go through any AI.

App connections

When it goes online

  • To check GitHub for new versions.
  • When you use features that need it: the Browser opens the sites you visit, the Library looks up game covers and info in stores, and My music downloads what you add from YouTube.

This website

masterdev.website

  • No cookies, no analytics and no accounts. Fonts are served from the site itself.
  • To show the latest version, your browser asks GitHub's public API.
  • Your browser remembers the language you picked (local storage, it never leaves your device).
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical visit data like any web server.

Contact

Questions about your data? Open an issue on GitHub.
