Privacy

Your data, plainly

MasterDev is built so your work stays on your PC. This is what leaves it, when, and where it goes.

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Stays on your PC

Your work and your local account

  • Ideas, projects, tasks, notes, voice notes, library, settings and themes are stored on your PC.
  • The local account (@username and password) only exists on your PC. The password isn't stored: only an encrypted fingerprint.
  • The local account locks the window, but doesn't encrypt your files: anyone using your Windows session can open them.
  • MasterDev has no ads and no third-party trackers.

Only if you join a server

Community

  • The MasterDev server you join stores your public profile, your friends, your messages and what you publish (games, playtests, reviews).
  • Your projects, ideas, tasks, library and settings are never uploaded.
  • People who look you up on the server only see your @username, name, picture and path. Your friends see your full profile.
  • Calls and screen sharing go directly between PCs (P2P), within the same network; they don't go through the server.
  • Playtest telemetry (how a test session is played) is only shared if you accept it for that playtest.
  • From your Gallery and Clips, only what you share is uploaded: a link (its images are deleted when it expires or you revoke it), the cloud according to your rules, a featured clip, comments and your game page's screenshots. Before uploading, MasterDev removes the metadata (location, your Windows user name).
  • From your music, only what you turn on is uploaded: your top of the month and featured playlist on your profile, your most played of recent weeks for mixes with friends, the songs you share in chat or in a music channel and the soundtrack you publish on Discover. When you remove it, it's deleted from the server.
  • You can host your own server: then the data stays on your machine.

Only if you connect them

Connectors: Discord, Spotify, Google, social, Twitch, stores and GitHub

Each connector uses your own developer app (you create it on each service's website) and does nothing until you paste its key and sign in. Without a key, it runs in test mode with sample data and nothing leaves your PC.

  • Your keys and tokens, encrypted on your PC. They're stored with Windows encryption (DPAPI, tied to your user) in MasterDev's local data folder, never in your projects. They're never shown, never copied into backups, never sent to any AI or to any MasterDev server, and they only travel to the service they belong to. Claude tasks that use connectors never receive a token: MasterDev makes the call.
  • Signing in opens the service's website in your browser (and comes back to MasterDev through 127.0.0.1, on your own PC) or gives you a code to type on its website. Each feature asks for its own permission only when you turn it on. “Sign out” deletes the token and revokes it on the service.
  • Nothing is published without you. Everything that publishes (a post, an announcement, a build, a Roblox place, an issue or a PR) shows you first what will go out and waits for your yes: for stores and Roblox, with a confirmation code; on Reddit, only with your click; on X, after you accept what it costs. Shorts and YouTube streams go out as private. Recipes start switched off and ask for approval.

What goes to each service

  • Discord. Your status (the game you have open, the project you're working on or “a secret game”, the song that's playing) goes to the Discord app on your PC, without internet, only if you turn it on. “Your account” asks only for identify (your Discord name). The bot, with the token you paste, posts the announcements, screenshots and clips you send, registers /bug and /playtest in the servers you choose, runs the bridges you create and reads mentions and messages from the channels you choose for the community summary (your AI writes that summary, treating those messages as third-party data).
  • Spotify and Last.fm. Spotify (no secret) reads what's playing, your playlists and your songs, receives your remote commands and saves the song you ♥. Last.fm receives the artist, title, album, length and time of each song you play in My music (scrobble), and your ♥.
  • Google and YouTube. Each feature asks for its own permission: YouTube reads your subscriptions and channel stats (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly) and only uploads a clip if you add permissions (youtube.upload), always as private. Drive uses only drive.file: MasterDev only sees and creates its own files (the MasterDev folder with your backups and the images you send), never the rest of your Drive. Gmail is read-only (gmail.readonly) and takes only what it needs: it looks for emails from the last 3 days from the stores on the list (Steam, itch.io, Roblox…), reads only their sender, subject, date and snippet (never the full body or attachments) and puts them in your Inbox with a link; if the rules can't classify one, the subject and snippet go to your AI (with a local AI they never leave your PC). It never sends, deletes or changes emails. Calendar reads your events and only writes to the “MasterDev” calendar it creates (calendar.app.created); Search Console and Analytics are read-only.
  • Bluesky, X, Reddit and Pinterest. Bluesky uses an app password (with no access to direct messages); X, Reddit and Pinterest, their own sign-in. They receive the text, images (without metadata) or clip of each post you approve and return its stats. Pinterest is read-only: it brings the pins from the board you pick into your references. “Suggest” or “Review with AI” send your draft (and, for Reddit, the subreddit rules) to your AI.
  • Obsidian. All on your PC, without internet: MasterDev reads the notes in your vault to link them with your ideas and, if you allow it, installs its plugin in it.
  • Twitch, OBS and going live. Twitch (public client, no secret) receives your streamer search and your list's ids to know who's live; each feature asks for its own permission (who you follow, read-only chat, clips, streaming). The chat never sends messages. When you go live, your video and audio go to Twitch or YouTube (private) with your stream key. OBS connects only on your PC or local network and never receives your password (just an encrypted challenge).
  • Steam, itch.io and Roblox. Steam reads your hours and achievements with your Web API key and, if you paste the partner key, your sales and wishlists (read-only). itch.io reads your games and, when you confirm, butler uploads your game's folder to the channel you choose; butler is downloaded from its official GitHub release only if you accept, checking its fingerprint. Roblox Open Cloud receives the place, the assets (images without metadata, audio, models) and the messages you confirm.
  • GitHub. Only the repos where you install your GitHub App (or those of your fine-grained token), with the permissions you give it: issues labeled bug (title and steps, without your PC's paths), Actions and, when opening a PR, the files that task changed on a new branch. Incoming webhooks go through your own MasterDev server, if you have one.

MasterDev doesn't sell or share the data it receives from these services, doesn't use it for advertising and only uses it for the features you turned on. Through “Sign out” or on each service's website you can remove its access whenever you want.

Only if you use it

Your games: library, saves, friends and discovery

The Library reads on your PC which games you have (Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox, Roblox, Minecraft and the ones you add) and how much you play them. That list, your shelves, notes, goals and lengths stay on your PC (the jugador folder in local data).

  • MasterDev doesn't put anything into your games. It doesn't inject code, read their memory or touch their files while they're running. The overlay is a separate MasterDev window on top of the game; the controller, sound and lights are adjusted through Windows and go back to how they were when you close the game. Games with anti-cheat don't allow mods.
  • Your saves and mods are only touched with the game closed and with a backup. Backing up, restoring, turning mods on or off waits until the game is closed; restoring shows you first what changes, saves what's there and can be undone, and turning off a mod leaves the folder as it was. MasterDev never deletes a game. Whatever a friend sends you is checked and installed with a backup first.
  • “Backup to Drive” (Connectors › Google) now also uploads your jugador folder (shelves, notes, goals, lengths…: only that data, never keys or tokens) to your own Drive, and your save backups only if you turn it on for that game.

What goes to each service (game ids only)

  • SteamGridDB (with your key): a game's Steam id or name to find its cover, which is downloaded once and kept on your PC.
  • Steam: the store receives individual game ids (genres, requirements, news, releases), never your list; with your Web API key, your SteamID for your hours, your achievements, your wishlist and, only if you turn on “See my Steam friends”, your friends list. Your Steam friends never go to the MasterDev server.
  • IsThereAnyDeal (with your key): the ids or names of the games on your wishlist, for their prices; if you sign in, it syncs that list with your Waitlist. Without a key, the names go to CheapShark.
  • Roblox: your public username (for your favorites) and experience ids; never your cookie or password.
  • Microsoft: nothing of yours; MasterDev downloads the public Game Pass lists and matches them with your backlog on your PC.
  • Also, only when you ask: the game's name to speedrun.com, the author and package to Thunderstore, and Ludusavi's save-path database from GitHub (checking its fingerprint). This week's free games come from Epic and GamerPower with nothing of yours. OpenRGB works inside your PC or network.

What your friends see

  • In Settings › Privacy › Playing with friends, all off by default: your hours for the week, your Steam games (without hours), letting friends join from “Playing now” and seeing your Steam friends. In Profile › 🎮 Status and player, seven switches, off by default, for your player card (favorites, hours, genres, featured achievements, top 10, goals and year). Only what's on is uploaded; turning it off deletes it from the server, and when you're invisible nobody sees anything.
  • Everything else only goes out with your click: a group you post, an invite to your game (without your IP; it expires after 4 h, when used or when you close the game), a game night, a challenge, an achievement race (only your percentage and the date of your last achievement), a recommendation or a journal entry.
  • “Watch my game” and “remote play” ask for your yes every time and go directly between the two PCs (P2P): only the game window, never the whole screen, and nothing is recorded. Your friend's controller only reaches the game window, only in games you mark as local, and panic cuts everything.

Only if you connect Claude

Claude

  • When you give it a task, Claude Code reads and edits that task's files and sends them to Anthropic for processing, using your own Claude account and under its terms.
  • The same with ChatGPT or Gemini, if you choose them: the task's files go to OpenAI or Google with your account. With a local AI (Ollama) nothing leaves your PC.
  • If you ask several AIs at once or ask for a second opinion, your question goes to each AI you pick. Generating or editing images with AI sends your description (and the image, when editing) to OpenAI or Google, only with your key and after telling you the cost.
  • In the Browser, the companion, “Explain”, “Ask” and the Lens send the text or image of the tabs you give them to the AI you pick; never private tabs or Claude’s tab. When Claude uses its tab it never sees your passwords: if a sign-in is needed, you do it.
  • In the Gallery, “describe with AI” is off by default; if you turn it on, each screenshot (scaled down) goes to Claude or Gemini so you can search by what's in it. Narrating a clip with an OpenAI or Gemini voice sends them only the text, with your key and after telling you the cost; the Windows voice never leaves your PC.
  • In your project's music, “Suggest music” sends your design document to your AI and “Generate” sends your description; the score it returns is played on your PC.
  • API and connector keys are stored encrypted on your PC and are only sent to the service they belong to.
  • The MCP server for other AIs (Connectors) is off by default. If you turn it on and paste its URL into Gemini or another AI, that AI can read your profile, projects, ideas and tasks (not your files) and, if you allow it, save things to your Inbox. The URL carries a private key; if you use the Cloudflare tunnel, requests go through Cloudflare. “New key” cuts access immediately.
  • The Community never uses Claude: talking with your friends doesn't go through any AI.

App connections

When it goes online

  • To check GitHub for new versions.
  • Only when you click “Download”: tools like ffmpeg, PresentMon or Ollama, from their official sites and checked to arrive complete. Recordings, their transcription and search all happen on your PC.
  • Only if you turn on phone alerts: the alert text (without paths, emails or keys) goes to ntfy.sh, to the private topic you create, or to the Discord webhook you paste. Anyone who knows that topic can read your alerts, so don't share its QR.
  • Only when you click “Download”: the official Roblox or Phaser docs, checked to arrive complete. If you share a skill with the community, it's uploaded to the MasterDev server you use.
  • When you use features that need it: the Browser opens the sites you visit, the Library looks up game covers and info in stores, and My music downloads what you add from YouTube.
  • In the Browser: converting currencies asks Frankfurter (from the European Central Bank) for exchange rates, with none of your data; if you turn on the shield, it downloads the EasyList and EasyPrivacy lists from easylist.to; “Save game” reads the public Steam, itch.io or Roblox page, and YouTube transcripts are fetched with yt-dlp. No account or keys needed.
  • In the Gallery and Clips: “To Discord” uploads the image or clip, without metadata, to the webhook you paste (stored encrypted); Real-ESRGAN, for upscaling, is downloaded from GitHub only if you accept; and “OneDrive and Google Drive” copies into your sync folder, which their own app uploads.
  • In music: “Recognize” sends the audio fingerprint (never the file) to AcoustID with your key, which is stored encrypted, and asks MusicBrainz for the song's details and Cover Art Archive for the cover. Searching YouTube and SoundCloud uses yt-dlp, and yt-dlp and fpcalc are downloaded from GitHub only if you accept, checking their hash.
  • The Gallery's “📱 Phone” opens a small separate server on your local network only while you use it: a new key in the QR code each time, only for addresses on your private network, with no access to the rest of MasterDev, and it turns itself off after 30 minutes. Windows will ask for permission: give it on private networks only.
  • Music mode's “📱 On your phone” uses that same local-network server with the same rules: it only serves your music, with no file names or folders, and turns off the same way.

This website

masterdev.website

  • No cookies and no analytics. Fonts are served from the site itself.
  • If you create an account, the site creates it directly on the official MasterDev server (api.masterdev.website): your @username, display name, path and an encrypted fingerprint of your password (never the password itself). You must be 13 or older.
  • While you're signed in, your browser keeps the session token and your @username (local storage). “Sign out” deletes them.
  • To show the latest version, your browser asks GitHub's public API.
  • Your browser remembers the language you picked (local storage, it never leaves your device).
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical visit data like any web server.
