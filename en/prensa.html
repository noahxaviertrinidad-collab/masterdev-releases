Short description

MasterDev is a free Windows app where you take your indie game from idea to playtest, and where other players try it.

Long description

MasterDev puts in one window what an indie developer usually has scattered: an idea notebook, projects in any engine with a design document and starter code that runs, a studio for each game with tasks, bugs, balance and a devlog, and a direct link to Roblox Studio. For players, it offers a showcase of games by real developers, playtests with notes and surveys, collectible cards and a community of friends. With Claude Code, optionally, it can hand off real work on the code. Your projects stay on your PC.
