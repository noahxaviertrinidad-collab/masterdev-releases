Install, update and uninstall

Everything MasterDev does on your PC, no fine print. Don't have it yet? Download it here.

Requirements “Windows protected your PC” Smart App Control Updates Portable Uninstall If you already had it

What your PC needs

  • Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit.
  • WebView2, the engine MasterDev draws its window with: the installer adds it if missing. If it's ever missing, MasterDev tells you when it opens, with Microsoft's official link (never a blank window).
  • 1 GB free on the disk (and about three times an update's size to download it).
  • No .NET and no administrator needed: everything is included and it installs just for your user, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MasterDev.

In the app, Settings › Updates › “Check my PC” checks all of it.

“Windows protected your PC”

If the version isn't signed (today)

  1. Open MasterDev-win-Setup.exe. The blue “Windows protected your PC” screen appears.
  2. Click “More info”.
  3. Click “Run anyway”. The publisher will show as “Unknown publisher”.

It happens with every new version until many people have downloaded it. It doesn't mean there's a virus: it means Windows doesn't know the file yet. Only download it from masterdev.website or the official GitHub and, if you like, check its fingerprint.

If the version is signed

Once MasterDev has code signing, Windows will show the publisher's name instead of “Unknown publisher”, the warning will appear much less (or not at all) and Smart App Control will let it through. Each version's notes say whether it's signed.

Smart App Control

It comes on in some PCs with a freshly installed Windows 11. It blocks unsigned programs with no “Run anyway” option. To check: Windows Security › App & browser control › Smart App Control settings.

If it's on, unsigned MasterDev won't open. You can turn it off right there (Windows warns that it can't be turned back on without reinstalling Windows: think it over) or wait for a signed version.

Some antivirus may give a false positive with a new unsigned program. If it does, check it on VirusTotal from the download page.

Automatic updates

MasterDev checks for a new version when it opens and every 6 hours, downloads it in the background (showing its size, and never on a metered connection like mobile data) and installs it when you close it. To skip the wait, press Ctrl + Shift + R: it updates right away and your tasks carry on where they were.

Every package is checked (its SHA fingerprint) before installing, and it never goes back to an older version. You can see and change it all in Settings › Updates.

The portable version

MasterDev-win-Portable.zip isn't installed: unzip it wherever you like (a USB stick, for example) and open MasterDev.exe. Everything lives in its folder: your data in datos and your projects in MasterDev. It also updates itself.

  • It leaves nothing in Windows: no shortcuts, no masterdev:// links, no scheduled tasks. That's why routines only run while MasterDev is open.
  • Connector keys are stored encrypted for that PC: on another one you'll have to connect them again.
  • To remove it, close MasterDev and delete the folder.

Uninstall

From Settings › Apps › Installed apps › MasterDev › Uninstall. The program, its shortcuts and what it left in Windows (scheduled tasks and masterdev:// links) are removed. Your data stays: it can't ask from there.

Or from the app, in Settings › Updates › “Uninstall MasterDev…”, which lets you choose: keep your data (the default) or send it to the Recycle Bin, and separately, with its own checkbox, your projects. You can export your data first, and a backup is always made.

Where your things stay

  • Your data (account, settings, library, music…): %LOCALAPPDATA%\GC3
  • Your projects and ideas: Documents\MasterDev

To delete them by hand: close MasterDev, paste that path into File Explorer's address bar and delete the folder (it goes to the Recycle Bin).

If you already had MasterDev

If you installed it before (version 1.0.x or with a friend's launcher), it keeps updating as always. Once on the new version, Settings › Updates offers “Switch to the new installer”: it downloads the installer, checks its fingerprint and opens it; when the new one opens, the old one removes itself. Your data and projects stay where they are. If you say no, nothing changes.
