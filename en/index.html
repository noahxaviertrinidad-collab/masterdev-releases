How it works

From a loose idea to a game other people test

  1. 1

    Write down the idea

    Keep your ideas in a notebook and turn them into a clear concept: what it's about, what you do when you play, and what it's like.

  2. 2

    Turn it into a project

    MasterDev creates the design document, the folders and starter code that already runs, in the engine you choose.

  3. 3

    Get players to test it

    Organize tasks and bugs, play your prototype and collect what testers say, all in the same place.

For creators

Your whole game, organized

First what makes the game fun; content comes later. MasterDev keeps you from getting lost along the way.

Available

Idea notebook

Jot down raw ideas, add images and references, and pick which one becomes a game.

Available

Projects in any engine

Templates for web games and Roblox, plus any other engine. Web games play inside the app, and you can add games you already have.

Available

Roblox Studio built in

See all the Roblox games on your PC, start one from a template and watch Studio live from the app.

Available

Tasks, bugs and voice notes

A board for each game, a bug list and a devlog. Record voice notes while you play and turn them into tasks.

Optional

If you use Claude, it works with you

With Claude Code installed, you can ask it for real work: build a mechanic, fix a bug or polish an idea, and watch which files it touches. It uses your own Claude account. Without it, everything else works the same.

Download

Ready in a minute

  1. Download the zip and unzip it into a folder of yours, for example Documents\MasterDev-app.
  2. Open MasterDev.exe. Nothing else to install.
  3. Create your local account and pick your path: creator or player.
  4. New versions arrive on their own: the app tells you and updates with Ctrl + Shift + R.

Windows will warn you

MasterDev is an indie project and isn't code-signed yet. The first time, Windows will show “Windows protected your PC”: click More info and then Run anyway. If you have Smart App Control turned on, Windows won't let it open until it's signed.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

The current version is a free download. Tasks with Claude use your own Claude account, if you choose to connect it.

What works today and what is still in testing?

Ideas, projects, Roblox Studio, tasks, bugs, voice notes, themes and cards work on your PC with nothing else. Community, Discover and playtests need a MasterDev server: the official one is in testing and invite-only for now. Calls and screen sharing only work within the same network.

What is a MasterDev server?

It's what connects people: friends, messages, published games and playtests. You can host one on your PC from Settings › Network, for your home or local network, or join one with its address and an invite.

Where is my stuff stored?

On your PC: projects and ideas in Documents\MasterDev, and your account and settings in your local app data. Only what you share in the Community goes through the server you join.

Is there a Mac, Linux or mobile version?

Windows only for now. Linux and a web version of the Community for phones are on the roadmap.

Which engines can I use?

Any you like. MasterDev ships templates for web games (JavaScript, HTML5, Phaser) and Roblox (Luau), and accepts any other engine per project.

Is it safe if Windows warns me?

The warning appears for any new unsigned program. Only download it from this page or the official GitHub; every version publishes its SHA-256 so you can check the file.
