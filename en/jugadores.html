For players

Play first and leave your mark

In MasterDev, games are made by real people who want to know what you think. Try them, tell them what happened and watch them change with your feedback.

Needs a MasterDev server

Discover, playtests and the Community run through a MasterDev server. Your MasterDev account already gives you access to the official server. Cards, profile, themes and music work without a server.

Needs a server

Discover and playtests

  • Games published by real developers, with reviews, tags and open playtests.
  • While you play, mark moments (“I got lost here”, “this is great”) and answer short surveys.
  • You get told when the bug you found is fixed.
  • Sharing how you played a session is optional: you decide for each playtest.

“I got stuck in the second room: I didn't see the lever.”

A playtest note like that is gold for the game's creator.

Available

Collectible cards

  • Welcome packs and daily and weekly missions.
  • Complete collections and make your own, even from your own game.
  • Trading with other people needs a server.

Available

Your profile, your themes, your music

  • A profile with picture, banner, board and your favorite games.
  • 12 themes with animated backgrounds, or your own colors and image.
  • My music, with a full-screen mode and a mini player.

Calls: local network only

Real friends

  • Friends, messages and groups with images. Always real people, never an AI.
  • Servers with channels, like the chat apps you already know.
  • Calls and screen sharing, for now within the same network.

Discord-style presence: online, away, do not disturb or invisible, with your status.
