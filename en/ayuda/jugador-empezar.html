🚀 Start

How MasterDev is organized and how to get around it.

In short: MasterDev has a menu on the left and a bar at the bottom for typing. Your account lives on this PC. If you just want to play, turn on simple mode.

  • The dock and ⊞ All

Your account #

Your MasterDev account lives on this PC: your @username, your name and your password. When you created it we gave you a recovery code: keep it, it lets you sign in if you forget your password. You can have several accounts on the same PC and switch between them with /cuentas.

The dock and ⊞ All #

On the left is the dock: just your favorite sections, large and labeled, like in an operating system. Drag them to reorder, and pin or remove any you want with the ★ in ⊞ All.

⊞ All (Ctrl+Space) opens the launcher: all sections, tools and widgets, with a search box. Type two letters and press Enter. If you prefer the classic menu, change it in Settings › Interface.

💡 In a narrow window the menu moves to a bar at the bottom with the most used items and “More.”

🎙 “Hey MasterDev” #

Press Ctrl+Alt+Space (or 🎙 in ⊞ All and in the Home search), say what you want (in Spanish) and let go: pon la radio chill, siguiente, llama a Leo, escribe a Ana que ya voy, abre la galería, busca trucos de Zelda, guarda el clip, recuérdame comprar el juego. Speech is converted to text on your PC (the same dictation as always, no internet). Anything it doesn't understand, it searches in ⊞ All.

💡 It doesn't listen all the time: only when you press the shortcut, and it stops when you stop talking.

Simple mode #

If you only want to play, simple mode hides everything about making games and AI, so you only see your own stuff. Turn it on in Settings › Interface, in your Profile or with /sencillo. Turning it off brings everything back as it was.

The bottom bar #

The fixed bar at the bottom (MD ❯) does two things: if you type plain text, it searches the web with the Browser; if you start with /, it's a command (/juegos, /musica, /muro…). Ctrl+K focuses it from anywhere, and /help shows every command.

MasterDev tabs #

Like in a browser, you can have several sections open in tabs at the top: Ctrl+T opens another, Ctrl+W closes it and Ctrl+Tab switches. ◫ puts two sections side by side, for example the chat and the Browser.
