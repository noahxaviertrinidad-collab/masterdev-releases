✨ The big update

The big update (builds 70–93), with where everything is and what you will use most if you make games.

In short: The big update changed a lot of MasterDev at once.

  • ChatGPT and Gemini as your main AI (1.0.3)
  • Servers and community (builds 96–99)
  • Your masterdev.website account (1.0.2)
  • MasterDev for everyone (build 100)

ChatGPT and Gemini as your main AI (1.0.3) #

In Settings › Main AI you choose who does your tasks: Claude (as always), ChatGPT (with OpenAI's Codex CLI and your ChatGPT account or an API key) or Gemini (with Google's Antigravity CLI and your Google account). Install the CLI, press Sign in and then Check: MasterDev never asks for passwords or keys.

You tell it your plan, and the model picker (in Settings and in each task) only shows that AI's models for that plan: on a free plan, only the free ones. If the CLI responds, you get the ones it lists for your account (codex debug models, agy models).

What only Claude Code has (Roblox Studio over MCP, the Browser for the AI, skills, automatic handoff, «See and undo» and Analytics) isn't available with the other two: each task says so when it starts instead of failing, and Analytics keeps using Claude.

Servers and community (builds 96–99) #

Servers: templates with roles and points (new “Game community”), 🚀 First steps for the owner, drawn or uploaded badges next to names, roles earned automatically, @everyone and @here with permission, quests that award points, auctions in the shop, ⚙ › Statistics and a 🎮 game channel that posts devlogs, versions and playtests.

Community: @mentions in wall comments reach 📥 Mentions, statuses that clear themselves, “Playing now” on your profile (if you turn it on), pinned messages in private chats, 🔎 Search my chats, 🎙 voice notes, a voted queue in 🎉 Together and your web game in the call's 🎲 Play, taking turns or all at once.

Your masterdev.website account (1.0.2) #

Accounts created on masterdev.website work in the app: in the welcome screen, “Sign in with it” creates the local account with the same @username and password and links it to the official server (https://api.masterdev.website). Invites to internet servers now work with the link or the code.

MasterDev for everyone (build 100) #

You can now build a public version for other people to download from GitHub Releases: a zip that runs without installing .NET and an installer that doesn't ask for admin rights. That version keeps everything in the MasterDev folder in your Documents, ships CLAUDE.md, the skills and the guides so tasks work from day one, and updates itself: it tells you when a new version is out, downloads it, checks it arrived intact and applies it with Ctrl+Shift+R. Your own copy stays exactly as it was.

Servers (builds 73–76) #

Set up your studio or game server with the “Indie studio”, “Playtesters” or “Jam” template in ⚙ Server settings (its icon or ⚙ in the chat header). Automatic posts for devlogs, builds and telemetry, and a secret address for GitHub, in ⚙ › Notices.

In ⚙ Server settings (its icon or ⚙ in the chat header): categories and drag-to-reorder, per-channel permissions with “View as this role”, a welcome with rules and questions, “Indie studio”, “Playtesters” and “Jam” templates, expiring invites, automoderation and slow mode.

Each server has its own points, levels, custom roles with an icon and a shop; forums, announcement channels other servers follow, custom emojis, events, stage rooms, polls and scheduled messages. Voice rooms come with chat, a status and a soundboard, and 🧭 Explore servers (or /servidores) searches by engine, genre and language.

Community (builds 77–79) #

Your friends see “Developing…” with your project and a ▶ Try button if it has a published game. Your devlogs and builds reach your followers’ 📰 Wall.

Click a name to see their mini card (with a private note). An emoji status that expires, @mentions with autocomplete and formatting with the Aa button. In Community: 📥 Mentions, Active now, 🔖 Saved and the 📰 Wall, which also reaches your followers, with 30–60 s clips.

In any call or voice room, 🎉 Together: listen to a song at the same time, watch a video in sync and play (draw and guess, rock paper scissors with your cards, and trivia). 🎚 Voice removes background noise.

Cards and packs (builds 80–85) #

In ✍ Create › Packs: a layered 🎨 Free canvas, your own shape and material, slots with exact odds and, in Opening, full-screen scenes with camera, lights, particles, a timeline and music. The card designer uses the same canvas, with custom fields and an animated back; 🌍 Community templates and Import .aseprite or .psd sit next to the starter templates.

In 🃏 Cards: 🔨 Auctions, a weekend pack, a card of the day with a streak, collection challenges and certified cards. In any card’s details, ⬇ Export saves it as PNG, GIF or WebM, and 🧬 Use as template respects the author.

Gallery (build 86) #

In the Gallery: ☑ Select several screenshots, ↔ before and after with a slider, a 📰 devlog with a caption per screenshot (to the wall or the Studio), a 🖼 showcase of 6 screenshots on your card, 👥 Shared with your chats’ albums, and the 🎮 filter by game.

Discover and store (builds 87–88) #

Your game page (trailer, screenshots, news, devlog and “who makes it”) is edited from the project page, with a preview, and you reply to reviews from it. Request the ✔ Creator badge in Profile › ✔ Creator.

Discover has tabs: 🛒 Store (rankings, filters, community tags, ⭐ Showcase of the week, ✨ For you and 📋 Lists), ♡ Wishlist, 📰 News and 🧪 Playtesters. Each game has its own page with a trailer, screenshots, devlog and reviews from people who played it.

Playtesting (builds 88–89) #

In the project page › 🧪 Playtest: ⚙ Configure (🎬 recording and markers, 🔒 closed test and 📡 live), 📅 Schedule, 📊 Summary with surveys and 🎬 Recordings and markers. With “📡 Live” you watch the tester play over the call, and their 😕 🐞 ❤ markers land at that moment of the recording.

Browser (builds 90–91) #

In the Browser toolbar: Chrome or Edge 🧩 Extensions (showing their permissions before installing), 🕒 Later to read later and 📖 Read with your font. In its ⚙, ☁ Sync between PCs (encrypted on your PC), and in a call, 👥 Browse together inside 🎉 Together.

Interface and accessibility (build 92) #

In Settings › Notifications: “All”, “Only mentions” or “Nothing” per server and per channel. In Settings › Interface, compact or cozy chat. Ctrl+/ shows the shortcut sheet, Ctrl+Alt+O puts the overlay on top of the game, and the Community server bar has folders and badges. In Settings › Accessibility, high contrast; rarities have a shape as well as a color.

Help, support and crashes (builds 70–93) #

In Guide › Help (or /ayuda): searchable FAQ, Write to us (the reply arrives as a notification), the rules, known issues and the bug hunt, which gives sparks for each confirmed bug. 🐞 Report a bug is still in the bar, and if MasterDev crashes it asks before sending the report, with no personal data (Settings › Diagnostics).

English (build 93b) #

In Settings › App › Language you choose Spanish or English, with a preview. What people write is not translated, and some parts may still appear in Spanish while we finish translating them.

Tournaments (build 93b) #

Knockout brackets for 4 to 32 people. On your server, ⚙ › Tournaments (prizes in its points); MasterDev ones, in Discover › 🏆 Tournaments (capped sparks). Both players confirm each result and, if they disagree, the organizer decides.
