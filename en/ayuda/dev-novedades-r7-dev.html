✨ Phase 2: AI, your way

Phase 2 (builds 122–128): AI works better and spends less. You see and stop each task, the queue understands dependencies and tests, the AI remembers your project, you track usage, create agents and triggers, combine several AIs and get alerts on your phone.

In short: Phase 2 tailored the AI to you.

  • Agents with long prompts
  • Connect Gemini or another AI to MasterDev (MCP)
  • See and stop tasks (build 122)
  • Queue and quality (build 123)
  • What the AI knows (build 124)
  • Savings and usage (build 125)
  • Agents and triggers (build 126)

Agents with long prompts #

In In the app: Customize › 🤖 Agents an agent’s instructions now take up to 60,000 characters (was 4,000), so full system prompts fit without losing the end. Paste it or press “📄 Load from file (.md or .txt)”; the counter turns red if you go over, and then nothing is saved. Note: they’re sent whole with every task that uses that agent, so a very long prompt costs more on each one.

Connect Gemini or another AI to MasterDev (MCP) #

In In the app: Connectors › MCP server for other AIs, turn it on and copy its URL (it carries a private key) to paste into Gemini's developer mode or any MCP client. The other AI can read your summary, projects, ideas and tasks and, if you allow it, save things to your Inbox; it never touches your files. A website like Gemini needs an HTTPS address: install cloudflared and press the tunnel button, or paste your own public address. “New key” invalidates the previous URL.

See and stop tasks (build 122) #

When you write a task you see its size, approximate cost and time («T2 · ≈ $0.40 · ≈ 6 min») and the context it carries: turn off what you don't need or press «Make it cheaper». A running task shows its steps and a bar with how full the chat is.

If a task seems stuck, «🛑 It may be stuck» appears with «Stop and summarize» or «Continue» (in Settings › AI you can choose «Stop on its own»). Right-click a screenshot in the In the app: Gallery › «Send to a task», and a long log pasted in the box gets trimmed (with «Send it whole»).

Queue and quality (build 123) #

Tasks can depend on others (if A fails, B waits and you unblock it), have a priority and each project its own number of tasks at once. ⚖ Two attempts runs the same task twice (even with two different AIs) and you keep the best one.

In the project sheet › Extra permissions, «Tests after each task» is detected on its own (npm test, a .sln or smoke*.ps1) and marks 🟢/🔴; Studio › Rules has a checklist and the linter. If a task changes code without testing it, «⚠ untested» appears; with 👎 you note it in Learning and «Explain it to me» tells you what it did.

What the AI knows (build 124) #

Each project has the 🧠 What the AI knows tab: the code map, the glossary, the decisions, the rules and «Pick up where we left off». In Studio › 📖 Glossary you note your game's names, and they only reach a task when you mention them.

⤓ Download official docs (Roblox, Phaser) so the AI doesn't make up functions (it flags doubtful ones with «made up?»), References › 🎮 Game to borrow something from another game, and in Settings › Learning 🧹 Review memory and ⤓ Export memory.

Savings and usage (build 125) #

In the app: Observatory › Usage: one card per AI with today and this week and, for Claude, the % of the 5-hour and weekly limits, with an alert past 80 %. On Mondays, 📊 Weekly report. If a big task comes in with the limit nearly full, the box offers «Wait for it to reset».

In a project sheet, 💳 Usage: the weekly cap, the files that use the most context (with «✂ Split it») and which AI gets it right most often, from your 👍/👎. In the night queue, back-to-back tasks of the same project go together («3 tasks in 1»).

Agents and triggers (build 126) #

In Customize › 🤖 Agents you create agents with their role, skills, AI and model and a permissions cap («🧪 QA» template) and pick them in the box. In ⚡ Triggers, an event launches a task («A task fails», «Critical bug»…): they start off and you test them with 🧪. Routines can carry an agent and conditions («only if there were changes»).

In Settings › AI › 🔌 My MCPs you add your MCP servers (Blender, Aseprite and Godot examples) and test them; they run on your PC, so only add the ones you know. Skills › «💡 What you repeat» suggests skills, and from PowerShell: masterdev tarea "…" -Json and masterdev esperar <id>.

Several AIs together (build 127) #

/preguntar … (or «🤝 Ask the AIs» on an idea) compares Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini in columns, and on a finished task you ask for a 🔍 Second opinion. ChatGPT and Gemini now understand Builds questions and plans. «⤓ For ChatGPT / Gemini» exports what the AI knows about a project, without secrets.

Ideas › ⤒ Import conversations turns your ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini export into ideas in the Inbox. Also: the 🌐 Translation agent in Studio › Translations, shared skills in Customize › Skills › 🌍 Community and the music chat (/dj) with the AI you choose.

Phone, voice and images (build 128) #

Connectors › 📱 Phone: alerts on your phone with ntfy (free, no account; scan the QR) or a Discord webhook. «Needs you» questions arrive with buttons, you choose what alerts you and set «Do not disturb» by hours.

With Ctrl+Alt+R say «MasterDev, …» (e.g. «MasterDev, fix the jump») and the task is created in the open game; «cancel that» undoes it within 10 s. 🎨 Generate concept in References and in Studio › Placeholder sprites, and ✨ Edit with AI in the Gallery viewer (with an OpenAI or Gemini key, ≈ $0.04 per image; it tells you first).
