🖼 Gallery and clips

Your screenshots and videos from any game on your PC in one place: capture them, sort them, edit them and share them.

In short: The Gallery keeps your screenshots and your clips. Take a screenshot or record a clip, sort them, edit them and share them.

  • Capture and record
  • View and sort
  • Edit
  • Your clips
  • Share

Capture and record #

  • Ctrl+Alt+S captures the game in front of you and Ctrl+Alt+G starts or stops a video. With replay on (Settings › Clips and recording), Alt+F10 saves the last few seconds of what just happened.
  • Screenshots from Steam (F12), the Windows Game Bar, NVIDIA and OBS arrive in the Gallery on their own, with the game they belong to.
  • In the Gallery's 📸 menu: capture with a delay, a saved area or window, and choose the sound and the notice. When recording you can put your camera in a corner, record vertical or audio only, and mark chapters with 🔖.

View and sort #

  • The In the app: Gallery is a timeline (jump to a month from the bar) and the grid has 4 sizes. The viewer goes from one to the next with ← →, compares two screenshots and shows their details (ℹ).
  • ☑ Select several (or Ctrl+A) to move them to a folder, tag them or delete them. What you delete stays 30 days in the trash.
  • Search by the text in the image, by color (🎨) or with #tag, and watch the ones you want as a Slideshow with music.

Edit #

✏ Edit opens an editor with layers (text, stickers, arrows, pixelate an area), filters (CRT, vignette, grain…), 🔍 Upscale and a GIF editor. Every change is saved as a version (🕘 Versions): you can always go back to the original.

Your clips #

  • In the app: Clips (in the sidebar) brings all your videos together: recordings, replays, Browser clips and calls. Group them by game, give them a title, description and thumbnail, and make collections.
  • The player goes frame by frame (, and .), from 0.25× to 2×, with an A–B loop (keys [ and ]) and full screen (F). 🖼 Use this frame saves it as a screenshot, and 👥 Watch together plays it in your call.
  • The clip editor cuts, joins, slows down, zooms and adds transitions, music and text, and compresses to 8, 25 or 50 MB so it fits on Discord.

Share #

  • 📤 Share: to Discord through a webhook from your server, copy the image, the MP4 or the GIF ready to paste in chat, or a link that expires (with a password if you want).
  • When sharing, MasterDev removes the hidden data from the photo (location, your Windows user name) and can add your watermark. ⭐ Feature puts a clip on your profile, and your friends comment on it at the exact second.
  • 📱 Phone (in the Gallery): turn it on, scan the QR code with your phone on the same Wi-Fi and you'll see your Gallery or send it photos. Windows will ask for permission: click “Allow” on private networks only. It turns itself off after 30 minutes.

💡 It works with any game on your PC, not just yours. The Gallery's ⚙ (or Settings › Application › Gallery) has all its settings together, and “⌨ Shortcuts” shows every key.
