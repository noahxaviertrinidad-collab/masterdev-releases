✨ Phase 5: music

Phase 5 (builds 146–150): music. For you, the same music players get (sound, library, music mode and friends); for your game, a soundtrack by zone, generated music and sound effects, Roblox audio, credits that fill themselves in and your soundtrack on Discover. All in the «Music for your game» chapter and, in the player guide, in «Music».

In short: Phase 5 brought music, for your game too.

  • Sound (build 146)
  • Library (build 147)
  • Music mode and mini (build 148)
  • Music for your game (build 149)
  • Social music (build 150)

Sound (build 146) #

The player's ⚙: a 10-band 🎚 Equalizer (per song, playlist, output or overall), ⏩ Speed without changing pitch, its own 🔈 Output and 🤫 Lower the music when you talk (calls, voice notes, dictation and clips with mic). Media keys and Windows controls with the cover art, the «♪ Music» group in Settings › Shortcuts (no keys by default) and, on opening, the song where you left it, paused.

Library (build 147) #

💿 Albums, ✨ smart playlists, 📁 folders with no playlist limit, 🧹 Duplicates by how they sound (fpcalc, with your permission), 🔎 Recognize with AcoustID (your key in Connectors; without it, test mode), ⚠ Review damaged files, 🚫 Banned, a 7-day 🗑 Trash, each song's quality and «✎ Edit song» with album, track, year, genre and BPM.

Music mode and mini (build 148) #

Search your library, YouTube and SoundCloud at once (with yt-dlp), ← → history, 🎙 Podcasts, 📡 Live and the ? guide. The ⧉ mini with three shapes, transparency, «Always on top» and a draggable queue; In the app: Settings › Sound and music brings it all together, and the «Now playing» widget has three sizes.

Music for your game (build 149) #

Studio › 🎵 Soundtrack by zone (tasks receive it), ✨ Suggest music from your GDD, 🎹 Generate music or sound effects for free (an AI score + MasterDev's synthesizer) and, in Roblox with Studio open, 🔊 Roblox audio. Studio › 📜 Credits and licenses saves CREDITS.md, each song can be 🎮 safe for games or 📡 for streams, and stream mode only plays the safe ones.

Social music (build 150) #

📊 Your music, 🤝 Mixes, importing and exporting playlists, 📱 your music on your phone, 🎵 Share song in chat, music channels on servers, 👎 and «⏭ Skip» in 🎉 Together, your top of the month on your profile and Soundtrack › 🎼 Publish on Discover: your soundtrack on your game's page.
