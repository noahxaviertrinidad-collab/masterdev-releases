✨ Fourth batch

In short: What arrived in the fourth batch of improvements.

Servers: your place and a list to join (build 68) #

When you create your network account you get your own server (“Server of …”), with #general and a voice room. It appears in the server list so anyone can join, unless you change it.

In Community › 🧭 Servers you see the servers on your MasterDev server with their icon, how many people are in them and how many are online. You can search, Join an open one, Request to join one that needs approval (with a message for the owner), or click 🎲 Join one with people so you're not alone: it puts you in an open one with the most people online. Full ones don't let anyone in.

In ⚙ Server settings (right panel of the chat) the owner sets a name, description and icon: an image or a design (emoji or initials over one or two colors: solid, gradient, dots or stripes). They also choose whether it appears in the list, how people join (Open, By request or Invite only) and the member cap. The owner and moderators accept or reject requests and can ban anyone they don't want.

You can leave a server whenever you want and create another (up to 3 of your own). If you're the last one left when you leave, the server is deleted.

Fixed: with Cards open, the mini player's video or cover art became huge and covered half the window (Settings, Community…). It now goes back to its normal size.

Your server, whenever you want (build 69): click its icon or ⚙ in the chat header (or ⋯ › Server settings). Under General you change how people join (Open, By request or Invite only), the name, the image or design, the description, whether it appears in the list and the cap. Under Channels you create, rename and delete text channels and voice rooms. Under Members you give roles (moderator or playtester), kick, ban, or transfer the server to someone else (👑). Under More you delete it for everyone. A guild of yours from before becomes a server via ⋯ › Convert to server in the list.

Cards: create without limits, a flippable album and more friends (build 67) #

Design everything by hand or by uploading images: the card's artwork, its background, the frame, the whole pack and its logo, the logo, cover, back and background of your collection's album, and even the opening animation. The pixel editor lets you pick the grid size (from 8 to 256 px) and make animations of up to 48 frames with onion skin. When you upload an image (animated GIFs too) you choose how many pixels it's converted to and how many colors it uses.

On each card you choose how much space the drawing takes (or to fill the window with no background) and its layout: Classic, Full art, Minimal, Landscape or Square. Packs come in 5 shapes (Classic, Wide, Box, Flap envelope and Pouch) and 5 openings (tear, explode, portal, dissolve or chest). Whoever creates a collection chooses the sound for its Legendary and Unique cards.

Flip-through album: pages of 9 sleeves per collection, with empty slots marked by number and rarity. Completing a collection gives its Master card (it doesn't come in packs). Each card shows “Copy 12 of 340”. There are Statistics (your luck versus the odds), History with “▶ Watch again”, achievement cards (finishing builds, fixing bugs, publishing a game) and collection achievements.

Season packs (Halloween, Christmas and the monthly Jam, only for a few weeks), open 5 or 10 at once (it only stops on Epic or better), gift a sealed pack and open live for a chat, where your friends react with emojis. Your mascot celebrates Legendaries and gets scared by Uniques.

With friends: wishlist with “Ask for it”, trade board for the whole server, counteroffer, live trade table, see a friend's album compared with yours, leaderboard, show a card in a chat, a notice on the Wall for your big openings (if you want) and a showcase of 3 cards on your profile.

Creating is easier: the collection editor goes through 5 guided steps (Basics · How the cards look · Cards · Packs · Publish), each setting appears only once and says what it does, and advanced options are folded under “More options”. Each pack is adjusted through tabs (Basics, Look, Opening, Odds), and the card designer goes through 5 steps (Drawing, Background, Shape and frame, Rarity and shine, Text). When you publish, whatever is missing has a “Go fix it” button.

For creators: balance warning in the editor, limited editions (pack cap or end date), reviews, sales dashboard, co-created collections with royalties split, export and import collections, collection from your game (with Claude) and cards for your playtesters. Trying community games in Discover gives sparks.

💡 Friends, store and copy numbers need your Community server on the new version; packs, album, design and stats work without it.

Cards remade and community collections (build 66) #

All cards have a new design: 12 frames (Metal, Neon, Gold, Parchment, Obsidian, Pixel, Holographic, Forest, Ice, Lava, Paper and Classic), 6 rarities with their gem and color (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Unique, with animated border), shines that follow the mouse (foil, holo, glitter, prism), pixel-art backgrounds and stat bars. Packs too: 10 papers and 5 finishes.

Opening a pack is no longer rushed: the pack drops, loads and tears open; the cards rise from inside, and each one stays in view until you click. Rare ones give you a heads-up before they flip (Legendary and Unique ones darken the room), and they burst particles in their color and rarity, big. If you prefer, Auto goes through them on its own.

✍ Create: your creator card with the card designer (draw your own pixels, import an image, choose frame, rarity, shine, background and the names of your stats) and your collections: from 3 to 40 cards and 1 to 3 packs with their design, their price, how many cards they contain, the guaranteed rarity and the odds of each rarity. They're published on your server and you earn 10 % of each pack sold.

🌍 Community: search for published collections, look at their packs and odds, and open them with your sparks as if they were from MasterDev. Their cards go to your album and can be traded with friends the same way. Trades, card gifts, kudos and guestbook were also fixed, since they weren't reaching the server from the app.

🎵 Your profile song now shows its cover art, on your card, on your friends' cards and in the list to choose it from (Settings › Profile › Profile song).

Settings and Profile, easier (build 65) #

Settings now has a column on the left with a search and pages sorted by category: Your account (My account, Profile, Privacy, Server and friends), Appearance (Theme, Colors, Background and effects, Interface, Sidebar, Accessibility), Application (General, Shortcuts, Sound and music, Notes and voice, Connections), Claude and System. Each page shows only its own options; at the bottom of the column, “Sign out”.

Profile is organized in tabs: Card (name, avatar, banner and colors), Style (name color and font, class, holographic, layers, song), Board, Status & player, Developer and References, with the preview always on the right. Claude's Learning is now in Settings › Claude › Learning.

When you open MasterDev, the connection to your server comes up sooner: it retries every 2 s at startup (and as soon as the server you host responds), and while it connects it remembers what your server can do, so buttons don't disappear.

Collectible cards (builds 60–61) #

New 🃏 Cards section (sidebar or /cartas): complete daily and weekly missions (sign in, play, listen to music, minigames, messages, ideas, tasks…) to earn sparks ✦. With them you buy packs: rare (100 ✦, 10 cards from the Workshop), epic (250 ✦, the ones from the Studio and the Workshop) and legendary (600 ✦, the 30). Each pack has 5 cards (duplicates are possible) with an animation when you open it; the rarest one comes out last. You get one free rare pack as a welcome gift.

The album holds the 30 cards of the first season with their rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare and Unique). Duplicates can be recycled for sparks or traded with friends in 🔁 Trades (you offer up to 5 and ask for up to 5; what you offer is set aside until they reply).

✍ My card: design your creator card (sprite, background, text, stats and colors; it comes from your profile) and gift it to anyone you like: they get a copy, and you always keep the original.

💡 Trades and gifts need your updated Community server; missions, packs and the album work without it.

Your identity and your board (builds 58–59) #

Profile › 🪪 Your identity: free name color (any color, gradient, shine and motion, no achievements needed), name font, creator class (Programming, Art, Level design, Music, Narrative or All-round), holographic card that shines with the mouse and layered banner with parallax. The profile song shows its cover art and plays 30 s with ▶ (it never plays on its own).

Board: ▦ Templates (Artist, Programmer, Streamer, Player, Minimal), profile in tabs (About me · Projects · Playing · Achievements), Devlog, Life timeline, My game's palette and 🪑 My room widgets (your setup in a pixel-art room you decorate), and “I want to play” with numbered covers.

How others see you (build 62) #

👁 How you're seen (Profile): who sees each part (Everyone, Friends or Only me) and “View as” a stranger or a friend. Your theme on your card: whoever opens it sees it with your background.

On a friend's card: kudos (Great playtester, Useful feedback…), cheers for their status with an emoji and their 📖 guestbook (you decide which signatures show on yours). Groups have a 🏢 Studio profile with logo, members, banner and games.

The interface, shaped to you (builds 63–64) #

Settings › Interface › 🧩 Your way: opacity and blur of the glass, corners, borders and shadows with sliders, sidebar on the left, right, bottom or floating, icon packs (Pixel, Line, Duotone, Emoji) and lock screen “Mine” (your background, a large clock, the quote of the day and your avatar).

Appearance: ✨ From a sentence… (describe a mood and Claude builds the background, colors, effects and music; you preview it before applying it) and 📅 Your style of the year (your most-used backgrounds, colors and effects, on a card to save or share).

Customization (build 55) #

Six new themes with animated backgrounds: Seabed, Night Dunes, Sakura Garden, Volcano, Meadow (light) and Crystal Cave. In Appearance › Theme you can filter (Dark, Light, From games, New) or click 🎲 Random.

Background effects (Appearance › Background effects), for all backgrounds, including your images and videos: animation speed, pixel size, 🎲 Another variation (rearranges stars, clouds, mountains…), blur, brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, vignette, CRT lines, grain and accent tint. There are one-click styles: Cinema, CRT, Dream, Noir, Vapor, Calm, Reading, Turbo and Retro. Double-click a slider to return it to its default value.

Colors: 20 quick palettes, custom Panels and Text, ↶ Undo / ↷ Redo, 🎲 Random palette (always readable), ☀ Light / 🌙 Dark version, accent 2 in harmony (complementary, analogous, triadic…), Matching base, copy and paste the palette and a global adjustment (rotate the hue, vividness and lightness of the base). “🖼 From an image…” also suggests background effects that match the image (Matching, Cinema, For reading or Vivid, with preview) or “Don't touch”. In the preview, “View as” simulates the most common types of color blindness.

The background generator now has 14 styles with thumbnails (aurora, synthwave, city, forest, mesh, low poly, bokeh, topographic, nebula, crystals, isometric…), colors from any theme, yours or random, scale, brightness, tone, pixelation with palette dithering, grain, vignette, mirror and text on top; formats 16:9, 16:10, ultrawide, dual monitor, square or vertical for mobile, up to 4K; six variations to choose from, ✨ Surprise me, Recent and “To the library”.

💡 Each background remembers its own: colors, effects, dimming, parallax, borders and zones. If you put “Noir” on Volcán and “CRT” on Jardín sakura, each one goes back to its own (even when the schedule changes the background). “⧉ Use on all” copies it to all your backgrounds, and “↺ Forget this background's settings” resets it to default. The community gallery publishes the theme without the effects.

Backgrounds #

In Settings › Appearance › Backgrounds and schedule you'll find “✨ Generate a background…”: draw a background with your colors, no AI or internet needed. “Use as background” sets it and “Save image” downloads it.

Three new switches, off by default: progressive focus (after 25 minutes in a row the background slowly darkens and eventually stops), background per project and season and jam (leaves at Halloween, snow in December, confetti at New Year's, sparks during a jam).

In the Community theme gallery: “Backgrounds only”, the “Most liked” order and a ♥ per theme. In Discover you'll see 🔥 Most used this week, with “Try 30 s.”

💡 Everything that moves respects “reduce motion” in Settings › Interface.

Your profile #

New banner: “My name pattern” (a unique drawing generated from your @username, the same on the card your friends see) or “Clip from my Screenshots” (a video or GIF of yours on loop).

“Streak” frame: it goes from ember to flame, fire and comet as you use MasterDev on consecutive days (3, 7, 14 and 30). Tiered achievements now carry their bronze, silver or gold ring.

New board widgets: Showcase (up to 4 images with their caption) and Activity (the heat map of your last 26 weeks). The achievements showcase supports a background color and is sorted with ▲▼.

“Share my card” makes a 1200×630 image of your profile to copy or save.

Browser #

Thumbnail on hover over a tab: it starts off; turn it on in Customize the Browser › Tabs.

▦ Tab board (Ctrl+Shift+Space): all your tabs as cards grouped by site, with search, close and drag.

The bookmark manager has the “Covers” view and the history has the “Timeline” view, split by sessions.

In “More” › Moodboard you open a panel beside the web with your images: drop in the ones you see or press “📌 Save this page.”

💡 Thumbnails are saved only on this PC (never from private tabs) and are deleted with the history.

Community, music and more #

Each chat or group can have its own background (🖼 button in its header), only on this PC.

In Settings › Sound: the ♪ icon pulses with the bass and music mode can use audio bars in the background. In ambient mode, a flash alerts you when something finishes (press C to turn it off).

Settings › Interface › Profiles: “High contrast” and “Low stimulation” with one click, and “Go back to what you had.” The theme editor has an accessibility report with “Fix all.”

📸 Capture mode (find it in ⊞ All, Ctrl+Space) puts any view full screen with a minimal interface; Esc exits. With your own server, “☁ Save as account theme” and “⬇ Bring my theme” carry your colors to another PC.
