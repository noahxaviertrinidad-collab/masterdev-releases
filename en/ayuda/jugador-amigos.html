👥 Friends and community

Real people, with their MasterDev account.

In short: To talk with your friends, connect to a server and add them by their @username. Then you can message them, call them and share your screen.

Connect to a server #

To talk with your friends, you all need to be on the same MasterDev server. One of you hosts it (on their PC or on the internet). In In the app: Community, without a server, you have three ways to join:

  • Join with invite: paste the link or code your friend sent you. It's the easiest way.
  • Servers on your network: the servers on your home or Wi-Fi network appear automatically; click “Use”.
  • Connect with the address: type the address you were given (for example http://192.168.1.20:47900).

💡 To host one on your PC: Settings › Network › Host, and check “Allow connections from the local network” so friends at home can join.

Add friends and chat #

  • /friend add @usuario sends a friend request; you can also do it from Community › Add friend.
  • Messages: images (📎, drag or paste), 😀 stickers and GIFs (save your GIFs with “★ Save GIF”), 📊 polls, replies to a specific message, reactions, editing and pinning.
  • Groups: a Guild 🛡️ has text channels and voice rooms, like a Discord server; a Party 🎮 is a quick group for playing today. Create them with /group create Nombre.
  • Sharing a music list: ↗ on a list in music mode; your friend can add it to their music.
  • ↗ Share anything (a screenshot, a song, a game, your theme, a high score…) with a friend, a group, your Wall or via WhatsApp, from the same menu.
  • Link preview: if you send a link, a card with its title and image appears. Turn it off in Settings › Interface.
  • 🎮 Games in common: in a group or with a friend, which games you all have and which is played most, so you can quickly decide what to play.

Calls, voice rooms and screen sharing #

  • 📞 Call or 🎥 video from the chat (/call @username), and 🖥️ share screen (/stream start).
  • Voice rooms: in Guilds, you join and leave whenever you want, without calling anyone. You can also see them in the Home widget.
  • ⌨ Push to talk: during a call, pick a key; the mic only opens while you hold it, even when you're in the game.
  • If you close the window during a call, the call continues: MasterDev stays in the tray (next to the clock) until you hang up.

The wall and levels #

In In the app: Wall you see what you and your friends post, with ❤. You can write and add screenshots, and with “Publish my updates” MasterDev only posts when you start or finish a game, set a record or unlock an achievement. By taking part you earn experience and level up (badges at levels 5, 10 and 20).

Discover and playtests #

In In the app: Discover you see the games that creators on your server publish, with “For you” (what fits your list). Each game has its own page: trailer, screenshots, requirements, the creator's news and reviews with stars. If the creator uploaded a version, ▶ Play downloads it and opens it. If you tick “I want to be a playtester” on your profile, you appear in the playtesters list and can try betas before anyone else.

📱 On mobile and Discord #

  • MasterDev on mobile: in your user menu (your avatar), “📱 MasterDev on mobile” shows a QR code. From your phone you can see your chats, friends, Wall and list (you need server 1.4 and to be on the same network, or a server on the internet).
  • Discord: in Settings › Interface › Discord, MasterDev sets your Discord status to “Playing…” or “Listening to…”. It needs the “Application ID” of one of your apps at discord.com/developers (its name is what people see).

Privacy #

  • Your status (online, away, do not disturb, invisible) is changed in your Profile. When you're Invisible, nobody sees that you're online or what you play.
  • From someone's card you can mute, block or report them. Images from people who aren't your friends are hidden in groups until you press “View.”
  • Talking with your friends never uses artificial intelligence.
