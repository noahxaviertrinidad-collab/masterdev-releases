🖼 Gallery and clips

Screenshots and videos from any game on your PC, and from yours: capture them, sort them by project, edit them, create store material and share them.

In short: Capture and record your game, edit the images and prepare material for your store or your socials.

  • Create for your game

Capture and record #

  • Ctrl+Alt+S captures the game in front of you, Ctrl+Alt+G records and, with replay on, Alt+F10 saves the last few seconds. Screenshots from Steam, the Game Bar, NVIDIA and OBS arrive on their own, with their game.
  • The Gallery's 📸 menu: delay, a saved area or window (with its own shortcut, “Capture framing”), a large capture in 4K or 8K of your web game and 📷 Studio. A screenshot taken with Roblox Studio in front saves the place and the camera: 🎥 Back to this view puts Studio back exactly there.
  • When recording: your camera in a corner, vertical or 1:1, audio only, chapters 🔖 and “⏺ Record the playtest”, which waits in Edit, records when you press Play in Studio and stops when you go back.

Sort and search #

  • The In the app: Gallery is a timeline and every project has its own Gallery tab. The viewer compares two screenshots (e.g. two builds) with synced zoom and shows their details (ℹ), including the build.
  • Search by the text in the image, by color (🎨, compared with your project's palette) or, if you turn it on in ⚙, by what your AI describes (off by default: it costs money). Tag a screenshot bug and create the bug in the Studio with its image.

The editor #

  • ✏ Edit: layers (text, sticker, arrow, pixelate), 50 undo steps, CRT, scanline, vignette, grain and curves filters, and your own .cube LUTs. Every save is a version (🕘) and you can go back to the original whenever you like.
  • For pixel art: scale ×2 or ×4 without smoothing, recolor with the project palette (bucket and eyedropper), remove the background of a sprite on a flat background and “Save as sprite” trimmed. For photos, 🔍 Upscale uses Real-ESRGAN on your graphics card (downloaded the first time, with your permission).
  • Batch edit (crop, size and format at once, with saved recipes) and a GIF editor with ping-pong and a target size.

Create for your game #

✨ Create brings 7 tools together: sprite sheet (PNG + JSON, and in Luau projects a .lua with the rectangles), collage “the same spot in 4 builds”, Steam capsule or Roblox thumbnail with your logo and color, A4 poster in PNG or PDF, press kit as a ZIP, devlog from a template and the palette of your screenshots as .gpl, .ase or .hex (to your Studio identity). “To the board” sends them to the references, and “🖼 Use as…” makes them a banner or a background.

Clips for your game #

  • In the app: Clips brings together recordings, replays, Browser clips (up to 5 minutes), calls and your testers' moments (they press F1–F12 in a playtest). By game, with title, description, thumbnail and collections that open back to back in the editor.
  • The clip editor has video, audio, text and music tracks, cut and join, slow motion, freeze, zooms, transitions and “Cut into pieces” by chapters. 📱 reframes to 9:16 following the action, 🎵 adds music with a license warning and 🎙 narrates with your voice or a Windows voice.
  • ⋯ More sends a clip to an idea, as a GIF to the GDD (Art section), to an animated card or to Studio › Game Feel. For Discord, compress to 8, 25 or 50 MB. Without ffmpeg, MasterDev offers to install it.

Share #

  • 📤 Share: Post to… X, Bluesky, Reddit or Discord in each one's format, copy for chat, link that expires (with a password, revocable in “My links”), watermark and no hidden metadata.
  • Which is better? creates a poll with several screenshots; 🌐 On Discover puts up to 8 on your game's page, and a clip can be its cover video. Your testers and friends comment on a clip at the exact second.
  • 📱 Phone: a QR code to see the Gallery or send photos from your phone on your Wi-Fi (click “Allow” on private networks only; it turns off after 30 min). The cloud uploads whatever your rules say, and 🗂 OneDrive and Google Drive copies a folder or an album to your OneDrive or Google Drive folder.

Photo mode #

In your web game (▶ Play), 📷 Photo mode in the game console pauses it, hides the HUD, moves the camera with the arrows or WASD and captures at the canvas resolution (or ×2) with the modo-foto tag. Esc puts everything back the way it was.

In Roblox nothing is injected into your game: copy this ModuleScript into ReplicatedStorage (or ask Claude in a project task to add it through the MCP). P enters and exits, right-click turns the camera, WASD and Q/E move it, and Ctrl+Alt+S captures.

-- ReplicatedStorage › PhotoMode (ModuleScript)
local Players = game:GetService("Players")
local RunService = game:GetService("RunService")
local UIS = game:GetService("UserInputService")
local CAS = game:GetService("ContextActionService")
local StarterGui = game:GetService("StarterGui")

local PhotoMode = { speed = 24 }
local conn, saved, hidden = nil, nil, {}

local function setHud(visible)
	pcall(StarterGui.SetCoreGuiEnabled, StarterGui, Enum.CoreGuiType.All, visible)
	local gui = Players.LocalPlayer:FindFirstChildOfClass("PlayerGui")
	if visible then
		for g in hidden do g.Enabled = true end
		table.clear(hidden)
	elseif gui then
		for _, g in gui:GetChildren() do
			if g:IsA("ScreenGui") and g.Enabled then hidden[g] = true; g.Enabled = false end
		end
	end
end

function PhotoMode.toggle()
	local cam = workspace.CurrentCamera
	if conn then
		conn:Disconnect(); conn = nil
		CAS:UnbindAction("PhotoModeSink")
		UIS.MouseBehavior = Enum.MouseBehavior.Default
		cam.CameraType, cam.CFrame = saved.type, saved.cf
		setHud(true)
		return false
	end
	saved = { type = cam.CameraType, cf = cam.CFrame }
	cam.CameraType = Enum.CameraType.Scriptable
	setHud(false)
	local K = Enum.KeyCode
	CAS:BindActionAtPriority("PhotoModeSink", function() return Enum.ContextActionResult.Sink end,
		false, Enum.ContextActionPriority.High.Value + 1, K.W, K.A, K.S, K.D, K.Q, K.E, K.Space)
	local rx, ry = cam.CFrame:ToOrientation()
	conn = RunService.RenderStepped:Connect(function(dt)
		if UIS:IsMouseButtonPressed(Enum.UserInputType.MouseButton2) then
			UIS.MouseBehavior = Enum.MouseBehavior.LockCurrentPosition
			local d = UIS:GetMouseDelta()
			ry -= d.X * 0.004
			rx = math.clamp(rx - d.Y * 0.004, -1.4, 1.4)
		else
			UIS.MouseBehavior = Enum.MouseBehavior.Default
		end
		local move = Vector3.zero
		if UIS:IsKeyDown(K.W) then move += Vector3.new(0, 0, -1) end
		if UIS:IsKeyDown(K.S) then move += Vector3.new(0, 0, 1) end
		if UIS:IsKeyDown(K.A) then move += Vector3.new(-1, 0, 0) end
		if UIS:IsKeyDown(K.D) then move += Vector3.new(1, 0, 0) end
		if UIS:IsKeyDown(K.E) then move += Vector3.new(0, 1, 0) end
		if UIS:IsKeyDown(K.Q) then move += Vector3.new(0, -1, 0) end
		local rot = CFrame.fromOrientation(rx, ry, 0)
		cam.CFrame = rot + cam.CFrame.Position + rot:VectorToWorldSpace(move) * PhotoMode.speed * dt
	end)
	return true
end

return PhotoMode
