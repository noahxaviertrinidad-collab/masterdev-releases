Cards and sparks #

In In the app: Cards you earn sparks (✦) with daily and weekly missions, open packs and complete the album. Duplicates are recycled or traded with your friends, and you can create your own card. Sparks are never bought with money.

Couch mode #

In the app: Couch mode shows your games full screen, with big covers and big text, and is controlled with the gamepad: d-pad to move, A to open, B to go back. It also opens by holding the controller's Guide button.

Your profile #

In In the app: Profile you change your card: name, avatar, banner, colors and the widget board others see. Your favorite games and your hours are only seen by your friends if you turn that on.

This guide #

Type what you want to know above, even as a question: “how do I record a clip?”. The search looks through both guides, the Help questions, the commands and the glossary, and shows you the best matches first with Read and Go there.

The Basic · Intermediate · Advanced filter shows only what fits your level, and ✓ Already used marks what you've already tried (it's saved only on this PC: it never leaves here).

Learning MasterDev #

The intro (/intro) asks how much time you have: Short (one minute), Long (everything) or Tailored (without what you don't use: no Roblox if you have no Roblox games; without AI, how to connect one). You can also watch just one part: /intro community, /intro roblox… If you close it halfway, next time it continues where you left off.

🧭 Show me (in every article and in the ?) takes you to the section, points at the real button and waits for you to press it.

The 💡 tip of the day in the Center shows you one thing you haven't tried yet (at most one a day; «Not interested» removes it for good, and you can turn it off in Settings › Intro). In Cards › Missions › 🎓 Learn there are one-time missions that tick themselves when you really do it, with sparks and a card at five. And in Profile › 🧭 Your map you see what you've mastered and what's left, area by area (only you see it).

Ask MasterDev #

In the ? of each section (or with /preguntar) type your question in your own words. MasterDev searches this Guide for the closest bits and an AI (Claude on its cheapest model or your local AI, whichever is set in Settings › AI) answers only from those, with Read, Go there and 🧭 Show me buttons. It never does anything for you: it doesn't start tasks, change settings or open outside pages.

With no AI connected it shows what the search finds. The AI only gets your question and the bits of the Guide: nothing from your projects, your profile or what you've already used. The conversation is cleared when you close it.

If you get stuck #

If something keeps happening (the same action fails three times, two tasks fail for the same reason —no Claude session, no limit left or no AI—, you go back and forth between two sections or search twice with no results), MasterDev offers you one specific bit of help: connect Claude with its “Show me”, ask, report the problem…

At most one notice every 30 minutes, and Don't show this again turns it off for good. It's all checked on this PC: nothing leaves it and no AI is used.

Learning paths #

In In the app: Guide › Paths there are short start-to-finish paths: your first web game in an afternoon (template, ▶ Play, change something with a task and ask for a playtest), your first Roblox game, publish on itch.io, play with your friends and record and share a clip.

Each step ticks itself off when you really do it (there's no “done” button) and has its own 🧭 Show me. Finish a path and you get a card from the rare collection.

Reporting a problem #

🐞 at the top (or /reportar) opens the report with an exact preview of what gets sent: title, what happened, version, how it was installed, Windows, WebView2, the already-clean log and the screenshot (with Remove the screenshot).

Before showing it to you, private things are removed: your Windows user name and the paths on your PC, emails, tokens and keys, your project names and the text of tasks and messages. Then you choose: Send to my server, Save as .zip (to send it yourself) or, once it's switched on, Open on GitHub (only the title and version go in the address). Nothing leaves without your click.

Help in the community #

If your server has an official help server (it shows ⭐ Official), in the ? and in In the app: Guide › Help you have Help in the community: you join with one click and ask in the channel for that section (#roblox, #musica, #navegador…). Bugs go in #fallos and ideas in #ideas.

Mentors 🎓 are people who already use MasterDev and want to help; the server's admin marks them. Ask a mentor for help opens a thread in #mentores: they answer you there, never in private unless you want. A mentor sees nothing more than anyone else, and can be reported like anyone else.

Show it to a friend #

In a call, 📽 Show MasterDev shares your screen (you pick the window, as always) and opens a 5-minute script next to it for your friend, depending on whether they're more into making or playing: what to show and in what order, with Next. At the end it sends them the website's download link in that same conversation.

The first-week survey #

7 days after you start, MasterDev shows you a small card once with 5 questions (what you came to do, where you got lost, what you looked for and didn't find, from 1 to 5 how useful it is, and a free comment). Not now leaves it for another day and No, thanks removes it for good.

It's truly anonymous: you first see exactly what gets sent (without your Windows name, paths or emails) and your server stores it without your @username or your IP.

MasterDev status #

In In the app: Help and support › Status you see whether your server responds and how long it takes, your version and the latest one, and the known issues. The same page is on the website: masterdev.website/en/estado.

The “?” in each section #

The ? button at the top (or F1) opens an explanation of the section you're in beside it, without leaving it. From there, Open in the Guide takes it full screen.

Shortcuts and commands sheet #

Ctrl+/ opens the sheet with all the shortcuts, grouped and searchable, including the Windows ones and the ones you changed (✎). 🖨 Print opens it as In the app: a page you can print. Every /help command has examples with Use, which types it into the bar.

In the bar at the bottom, start with ? to ask the Guide: ?cómo grabo un clip. /guia text works too.

Glossary #

The In the app: glossary explains MasterDev's words in one line (task, handoff, Needs you, sparks, Together…). In the articles, the first time one appears it's underlined: hover to see what it is.

Easy read #

Easy read (in Settings › Interface or with the Aa button in the Guide) uses larger, clearer text (Atkinson Hyperlegible), more line spacing and no italics, and shows an In short summary in plain words at the top of every chapter.

The language is changed in In the app: Settings › Language: the whole Guide is available in Spanish and English.

Help, bugs and support #

In In the app: Help and support you'll find the frequently asked questions, the bugs we already know about and how to write to us. 🐞 (at the top) prepares a report with the screenshot and the version: you see what goes in it before it's sent.

Settings #

In the app: Settings is organized in groups: your look (theme, interface, sound), your account and its privacy, the network and servers, the AI, shortcuts, language, updates and your data (daily backups and “Export my data”). It has its own search at the top.
