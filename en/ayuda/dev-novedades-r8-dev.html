✨ Phase 3: a Browser for creating

Phase 3 (builds 129–134): the Browser, made for creating. Tabs you can rename, stack and set to expire, an address bar that calculates and searches all your tabs, tools to test your web game, a board for your references, your AI beside you while you browse, a shield against ads and containers for another account.

In short: Phase 3 brought a Browser for creating.

  • Tabs (build 129)
  • Address bar and search (build 130)
  • Tools for your game (build 131)
  • Research and save (build 132)
  • AI in the Browser (build 133)
  • Performance and order (build 134)

Tabs (build 129) #

Right-click a tab: “✏ Rename…” (name, emoji and color), “⏳ Expires in…” (it closes or moves to “Read later”), “Stack this site’s tabs”, “Move to its own window” and “Game · docs · console (in three)”.

The ▦ Tab board (Ctrl+Shift+Space) shows all your tabs by group, space or site, with live thumbnails; Ctrl + click picks several to close or move them. In History › “🗂 Tabs” you get back what you had open this morning or undo “close the others”.

Address bar and search (build 130) #

Type 2^10, 5 km en mi, #ff8800, hora en Tokio or 20 usd en eur in the address bar and the answer appears instantly (Enter copies it). ? your question asks your main AI and @pestañas text searches all your open tabs. New shortcuts for devs: ch, df, ph, lo and un.

In Browser settings › Start and search you create short links and import your search engines from Edge or Chrome. History opens as a timeline with filters and, if you turn it on in Privacy, saves the text of pages so you can search it. Right-click a bookmark: “🏷 Tags…” and “✨ Smart folder…”.

Tools for your game (build 131) #

Open your web game in the Browser and its 🧰 Console: when you save a game file, the tab reloads by itself at the same spot (“↻ Reload on save”). “⏺ Record 30 s” leaves a report in Studio › 📈 Performance (pick two to compare them) and the “🐢 Network” menu simulates 3G, 4G, high latency or offline.

Every error comes with “🐞 Bug” (to Studio with a screenshot and your last steps, never what you type) and “🛠 Fix it” (opens a pre-filled task, it doesn’t launch it). “🧱 Loaded assets” flags what’s heavy, and “📐 Six sizes” and “🎮 As on itch.io” show your game on other screens and on a store page.

Research and save (build 132) #

In the References of an idea or a project (or in the Browser’s moodboard), “🧭 Board” lays out your references on a canvas with colored notes and arrows: drag cards, links, images or text.

⋯ › “💾 Save the page” as PDF, web page or full-length screenshot. On a Steam, itch.io or Roblox game page you get “🎮 Save game” (and “📊 Analyze the competition”); on YouTube, “⏱” saves the moment with your note. “Read later” has “⏰ Remind me”, tags and the weekly list; when highlighting, hover the margin mark to write a note, and “Harvest assets” flags sprite sheets and warns when the license is unknown.

AI in the Browser (build 133) #

In the sidebar’s “Panels” group, 🧭 opens the companion (Ctrl+Shift+.): a chat with your AI about the page you’re viewing, saved per space (it never reads Claude’s tab or private tabs). “＋ Panel” puts Discord, Spotify, the Roblox docs or any site in a column on the right.

Select text → “🧠 Explain” explains it for your project. 💬 Ask reads up to 20 tabs, YouTube videos and PDFs, and its citations take you to the highlighted sentence. The Lens pulls out the palette, style and font and you save them to a project.

When Claude uses its 🤖 tab: “✋ Take control” pauses it, and if it asks you for something (“Claude asks you: sign in…”) you do it and press “✓ Done”; it never sees passwords. In Studio › Store page, “🛒 Fill in on Steam / itch”: Claude fills in the page and never presses save or publish.

Performance and order (build 134) #

The ⋯ menu is split into blocks (Page, Tools, Data, Help) with a search box on top, and at the end of the sidebar ✏ reorders it, hides buttons or picks a profile: “Code”, “Play” or “Research”. 📊 Performance shows what each tab uses (put it to sleep or close it).

In ⚙ › Shield you turn on EasyList and EasyPrivacy and check the 🛡 Report. Right-click a tab or a link › “Open in container” (Roblox 2, Discord 2…) for another account on the same site. ⚙ › History › “⬇ Import…” brings history, search engines and bookmarks from Edge, Chrome or Opera GX (with “Undo”). On In the app: Home, the “Browser” widget (“Read later”, downloads and the 🤖 tab), and the daily backup now saves how your Browser is set up (never cookies or history).
