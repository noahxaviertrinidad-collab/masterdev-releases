🌐 The Browser

A full browser, without leaving MasterDev.

In short: The Browser is a web browser inside MasterDev, with tabs, bookmarks and an ad shield.

Player stuff #

The new tab shows you 🔴 Live (your streamers), 🎁 Free (this week's free games), 🏷 Deals and 📰 News. Change it in ⚙ › New tab (“If you're a gamer”, “Always” or “Never”).

💡 Put a video or live stream in ⧉ Floating and play with it on top.

Translate and watch later #

  • Translate: in the ⋯ menu (or right-click the tab) › “Translate to Spanish”. “View original page” restores it as it was.
  • 🕒 Watch later: right-click the tab › “Watch later”. It stays in the Home widget with its thumbnail, and if you haven't looked at it for days, it reminds you.

Open it and move around #

The Browser is a real web browser inside MasterDev (the same engine as Edge). Open it from the menu, with /navegador or by typing an address in the bar below. Your tabs are saved when you close MasterDev.

  • Address bar: type a website or whatever you want to search for. As you type, suggestions appear: what Enter would do, your bookmarks ★, your history 🕘 and open tabs ⇄. Ctrl+L or F6 takes you to it.
  • Search shortcuts: start with a short keyword to search a specific site: yt gatos searches YouTube, steam, itch, wiki, tr (translator)… You can change them or add your own (up to 30) in ⚙.
  • Back, forward and reload: ← → ⟳ in the bar, or Alt+← Alt+→ F5.
  • Zoom per site: Ctrl Ctrl+- Ctrl+0 (or the mouse wheel with Ctrl). MasterDev remembers each site's zoom; the “125 %” chip in the address bar resets it to normal.

Tabs #

  • ＋ or Ctrl+T opens a new tab; Ctrl+W closes it and Ctrl+Shift+T reopens the last one you closed.
  • Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab switch tabs; Ctrl+1…8 goes to that tab and Ctrl+9 to the last one. Drag them to reorder.
  • Right-click a tab: reload, duplicate, pin (it stays in front, small and without ×), mute, add to bookmarks or quick links, close the others… The 🔊 shows which one is playing.
  • 🕶 Private tab (Ctrl+Shift+N): leaves no history or saved zoom, and isn't remembered when you close it.
  • ⌄ Search tabs (Ctrl+Shift+A) lists all open tabs. Tabs you haven't looked at for a while go to sleep 💤 to save memory, and when you return they're just as you left them (tabs playing sound never sleep).
  • In ⚙ › Tabs you can put them vertically on the left, change their style and group them by color.

The new tab #

When you open a new tab you see the MasterDev start page: a large search box, your quick links (up to 12) and, if you want, the time and a greeting. ⚙ Customize this page changes what it shows and its background (your theme's, a color or an image of yours).

Bookmarks, history and downloads #

  • ☆ in the address bar (or Ctrl+D) saves the page to bookmarks, which appear in the bar below. You can make folders, rename by double-clicking, and import them from Edge or Chrome via ☰ Manage bookmarks. Ctrl+Shift+B shows or hides the bar.
  • 🕘 History (Ctrl+H): with search, by day and with “Delete…”. You decide how many days are kept (or none) in ⚙ › Privacy.
  • ⬇ Downloads (Ctrl+J): what you download, with its progress, “Open” and “Folder”. Clearing the list never deletes the files.

The tools on the left #

On the left of the page there's a toolbar (in narrow windows it's in the ⋯ menu):

  • ✂ Crop (Ctrl+Shift+S): the page freezes and you choose what to crop (rectangle, freehand or everything). It's saved straight to Pictures\MasterDev\Capturas and you copy or download it from there. 🖼 Gallery shows them all.
  • 🎥 Record: a clip or GIF of the tab, up to 15 seconds.
  • ⧉ Floating: pops the tab's video into a small window on top of all apps, including a game.
  • 📖 Reader: just the article text, in your theme's colors.
  • 🖌 Styles: changes how a site looks (dark mode, larger text or your own CSS).
  • 🎨 Resources: the page's palette, fonts and images.
  • 💾 Sessions: saves a group of tabs under a name and reopens it whenever you want.
  • ⫿ Split: two tabs at once, side by side. Drag the border to share the space.

Shield, privacy and permissions #

  • 🛡 Shield: blocks ads and trackers. Its menu shows how many things it blocked on that page and lets you turn it off for a specific site if something doesn't work.
  • Permissions: when a website asks for camera, mic or location, MasterDev asks you first. In ⚙ › Websites you can see what you gave each site and remove it.
  • Clear browsing data (⚙ › Privacy): cookies, cache, history, passwords…, from the last hour to everything.
  • 📱 Device mode (Ctrl+Shift+M): view the web as on a phone, a tablet or any size you want.

Customize it #

Everything is changed from the ⚙ at the end of the bar (Ctrl+, or /navegador ajustes): the search engine, what opens at startup, tabs, which buttons show, the theme for websites, the starting zoom, privacy, shortcuts… Each setting has its ↺ to go back to the default, and it saves automatically.

💡 Your Browser settings, history and bookmarks belong to this PC: all your MasterDev accounts share them.
