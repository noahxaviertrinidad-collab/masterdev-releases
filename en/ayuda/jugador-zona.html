🏠 Your area (Home)

The start screen, with your things in view.

In short: Home is your start screen. Add the widgets you want and remove the ones you don't.

  • Your desktop
  • Widgets
  • Alerts
  • Make it yours

Your desktop #

At the top of Home you have the time, a greeting and a search box that opens ⊞ All. Below, the widgets with space between them, so everything is easy to see at a glance.

Widgets #

  • Keep playing: the game you have open and the last ones you played, with ▶.
  • Now playing: the song that's playing with its controls, your playlists and music mode.
  • Friends online: who's connected and what they're playing or listening to.
  • Voice rooms: the voice rooms of your groups and who's inside; Join with one click.
  • Profile, Discover and Library (everything MasterDev found on your PC).
  • 📅 Calendar: your reminders, releases you're waiting for and meetups with friends; it alerts you on time (and 10 minutes before a meetup). It also shows until when a game is free.
  • 📝 Notebook: notes and a to-do list for each game (codes, routes, where you left off). Also from the 📝 on each game.
  • 🕒 Watch later: the websites and videos you saved in the Browser, with their thumbnail.
  • 📊 Performance: CPU, GPU and memory usage, in a small size so it doesn't take up space.
  • 🧩 My widgets: create your own without coding (note, links, countdown, world clocks, counter, list or a “What should we play?” wheel) and share them with a friend: they get it with an “Install” button.

Alerts #

The 🔔 at the top gathers everything that happens (messages, calls, friends, achievements, deals…), grouped by day and with tabs by type. You can mute a whole type and set Do not disturb hours.

Make it yours #

  1. Click ▦ Customize at the top of Home.
  2. Drag widgets to reorder them and choose their size (S, M or L).
  3. Remove the ones you don't use or add others with ＋ Add widget.

💡 Some widgets can also be pulled out to the Windows desktop, so you can keep them in view with MasterDev minimized.
