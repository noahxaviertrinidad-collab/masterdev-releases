✨ Phase 6: connectors

Phase 6 (builds 151–158): external connectors. Discord, Spotify, Last.fm, YouTube, Google, Bluesky, X, Reddit, Pinterest, Obsidian, Twitch, OBS, Steam, itch.io, Roblox Open Cloud and GitHub, each one in In the app: Connectors with your own keys (encrypted on your PC). Without a key, everything runs in test mode; whatever publishes always asks for your yes. All together in the "Connectors" chapter.

In short: Phase 6 brought connectors: Discord, Google, socials, Twitch, stores, GitHub and recipes.

  • Discord 1 (build 151)
  • Discord 2: the bot (build 152)
  • Spotify, Last.fm and YouTube (build 153)
  • Google (build 154)
  • Social and notes (build 155)
  • Twitch and streams (build 156)
  • Steam, itch and Roblox (build 157)

Discord 1 (build 151) #

Connectors › Discord in three parts: Status (working on your project, "a secret game" or nothing depending on the project; playing; listening; with priority, buttons and "🔕 Focused"), Your account (sign-in in the browser) and Bot. Screenshots and clips go to the bot's channel as well as the webhook, 📨 Invite Discord friends and, in a jam, 📅 Discord event. Underneath, the pieces for the whole phase: sign-in with PKCE or with a code, tokens that renew themselves and test mode.

Discord 2: the bot (build 152) #

Connectors › Discord › Bot: 📣 Announcements (build, version, devlog, playtest and screenshot, with preview), ⌨ /bug and /playtest in the servers you tick (/bug arrives as a "From Discord" draft you accept in the Studio), 🔗 Bridges between a channel on your MasterDev server and a Discord one, @ Mentions to the Inbox and 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Community, which summarizes what people say and suggests replies. Connectors › Mobile gains "By direct message from the bot". They answer while MasterDev is open.

Spotify, Last.fm and YouTube (build 153) #

🎵 Spotify in music mode and its row in the player and the mini (no secret: just the Client ID), Last.fm with scrobbles and ♥, ⇩ Bring in a playlist from Spotify or YouTube, covers at 2048 in Settings › Sound and music › Covers, and gamedev subscriptions to "For later". With your Google client: Analytics › ▶️ YouTube and "Upload as Short", which uploads private (you publish it in YouTube Studio).

Google (build 154) #

Connectors › Google › Add permissions, each feature with its own: Drive backup (each daily backup to MasterDev/Copias and "Restore from Drive", only with drive.file), store emails to the Inbox (Gmail read-only), website stats (Search Console and GA4 in Analytics › 🌐 Web) and the In the app: 📅 Calendar view with your launch. 📅 Schedule in Clips and the Gallery: it goes out on time while MasterDev is open; if it was closed, it asks you.

Social and notes (build 155) #

✍ Post to Bluesky (with an app password) and to X (with what your plan charges per post), 👽 Reddit draft that only goes out with your click, 📌 Pinterest in References, 📊 Published clips with how each one did, and 🎞 Trailer for the store. Obsidian in depth: 📓 Related notes on each idea and 🧩 Plugin with the MasterDev panel in your vault.

Twitch and streams (build 156) #

Twitch with a code, features with their own permission (who you follow, chat, clips, streaming): 📺 Live (/directos) and 📺 Live now on your games' pages and in Discover. /streamer (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S) blurs your data and puts the chat and "✂ Clip" in a corner. OBS over WebSocket (it knows whether you're streaming or recording) and 🔴 Go live to Twitch or YouTube (private) with ffmpeg.

Steam, itch and Roblox (build 157) #

Steam: sales and wishlists with your partner key (read-only) in Analytics › 🎮 Steam and the 💰 Sales widget, reviews to the community agent and the Inbox, and hours and achievements on your Profile. Studio › 🚀 Stores and publishing: itch.io (your key moves to Connectors on its own; ⬆ Publish with butler and the prepared devlog) and Roblox Open Cloud (💾 Save or 🚀 Publish the place, upload images, audio and models and "Insert into Studio", 📢 Message the servers). Everything that publishes asks for a confirmation code. butler downloads the first time, with your permission, and MasterDev checks its fingerprint before installing it.

GitHub and recipes (build 158) #

Connectors › GitHub with a code (or a fine-grained token): each project's repo, Issues ↔ bugs from the Studio, ⚙ GitHub in Builds (Actions) and ⬆ Open PR on a finished task. 📥 Incoming webhooks through your server (if you have it on a VPS), 🔌 Tools for tasks (the connectors MCP) and In the app: Customize › 🧪 Recipes: "if this happens, do this" across connectors, switched off when created and with approval in Needs you.
