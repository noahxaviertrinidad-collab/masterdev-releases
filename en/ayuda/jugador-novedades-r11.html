✨ Phase 7: for players

Phase 7 (builds 159–165): for players. All your games in one library with covers and ▶ Play, your saves and mods with backups, an overlay on top of the game, more ways to play with friends, finding what to play and at what price, and your achievements and memories. MasterDev never touches a running game or puts anything inside it.

In short: The latest: all your games in one library, an overlay on top of the game, more ways to play with friends and your achievements in one place.

Your library (build 159) #

In the app: Games › Your games brings Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox, Roblox and Minecraft together in a ▦ Covers grid with ▶ Play (also /jugar <nombre>). Right-click a game for its status, its shelves, its cover («🖼 Change…», with your free SteamGridDB key in Connectors, or «Upload mine») and its length. Your hours from each store are combined without counting twice, and 🎲 What should I play? picks one for you.

In the 🧱 Roblox row, type your Roblox username: your recent and favorite experiences, and «Let my friends join…» so they can get into your server.

More library (build 160) #

＋ Add game: an .exe or your retro games folder with your emulator (MasterDev doesn't download emulators, BIOS files or games). On each game's page, 💾 Your saves: dated backups (only with the game closed, also automatically when you close it, to Drive or a folder of yours) and «Restore», which shows what changes, saves what's there first and can be undone.

The Mods tab (Thunderstore or a zip of yours, by profiles; turning them off leaves the folder as it was), each game's 📰 News, 💽 Space (how much each one takes; MasterDev never deletes a game) and 🛋 Couch mode to play with a controller (or hold the Guide button).

While you play (build 161) #

With the game in borderless window, Ctrl+Alt+O opens the overlay with 5 tabs: 💬 Chat, 📝 Notes, ⏱ Timer, 📊 Performance and 🎚 Sound. Game notes with time and screenshot, a speedrun timer with splits and your best time (import your .lss; Ctrl+Alt+F9…Ctrl+Alt+F12 only while it's open) and Ctrl+Alt+D to dictate to a friend (it's only sent with your click).

🎚 Game sound (your friends' voices on top), In the app: 🎮 Controllers (test, dead zone, vibration and a profile per game), In the app: 💡 Lights with OpenRGB that follow the game, ⚙ Recommended graphics › Read my PC on the game's page and a 📔 journal when you close a game after 10 minutes. Everything goes back to how it was when you close the game.

With friends 1 (build 162) #

In In the app: Settings › Privacy › Playing with friends you choose what your friends see (all off by default). Community › Friends › 🎮 Playing now (your Steam friends too) and 🔎 Find a group by game, language, schedule and level. 🔗 Invite to my game on each game's page: your friend clicks the link, sees what will open and joins (Steam with your lobby, Roblox with your permission, Minecraft).

In a group's ⋯ menu: 🗓 Game nights (you vote on game and time, with reminders), ⚔ Challenges with a leaderboard and 🎮 Games in common. In Discover, 🎮 Clubs for each game.

With friends 2 (build 163) #

💌 Recommend to… sends a game card to the chat with your note and your stars (with «♡ To my list»). In «Playing now», 👀 Watch a friend's game (it asks for permission and shares only the game window, with reactions) and 🎮 Remote for a local game: Remote Play Together if it's on Steam, or your friend plays with their controller from home. Panic: the overlay shortcut or holding Guide cuts everything.

Also: positional voice in calls (each voice in its place and the leader's on top), sending a save or a mod to a friend (installed with a backup first) and minigames while you wait for the others.

Discover (build 164) #

In the app: Discover › ✨ For you learns from your hours and your stars («Not interested» teaches it too). 🏷 Deals: a single wishlist with the best price in official stores and the historical low (with your IsThereAnyDeal key), this week's free games by store and Game Pass (what's leaving that you still have pending).

📅 Releases with reminders (and to your Google Calendar), 🎮 Ready to play (finished games from MasterDev devs) and 📰 News › News from your games. On Home, the 🎁 Free this week, 📅 Coming soon and 📰 News from your games widgets.

Achievements and memories (build 165) #

In the app: Games › 🏆 Achievements: all your Steam and MasterDev achievements on a timeline, ☆ to feature 6 and 🏁 races with your friends. 📔 Journal with what you finish and your rating, 🎯 Goals with streaks, Cards › 🎮 Your games (earned by playing) and 🎉 Your year of gaming in December. With instant replay on, «🎬 Save a clip when I unlock an achievement».

Profile › 🎮 Status and player: your favorites, your hours, your top 10 and «Share card»; your friends only see what you turn on. If you're a player, MasterDev opens on Games (switch with /modo creador or /modo jugador).
