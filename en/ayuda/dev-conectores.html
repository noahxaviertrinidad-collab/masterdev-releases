🔌 Connectors

Your Discord, Spotify, Google, social, Twitch, OBS, store and GitHub accounts, connected to your projects through your own developer apps. All in In the app: Connectors.

In short: Connect MasterDev to your accounts (Discord, GitHub, Google, Steam…). Your keys are stored encrypted on your PC.

  • How they connect
  • Test mode and nothing without you
  • Launch and community
  • For your tasks and recipes

How they connect #

Each service asks you to create your own app on its website (almost always free) and paste its ID or key only in Connectors, never in a chat or a task. They're stored encrypted on your PC (Windows DPAPI) and never sent to the AI. Then "Sign in" opens the browser (it comes back to MasterDev on its own) or gives you a code to type on the service's website. Each feature asks only for its own permission, and "Sign out" revokes it.

Test mode and nothing without you #

Without a key, each connector runs in test mode: you see how it would look and anything it would publish stays "prepared". With a key, everything that publishes asks for your yes before it goes out: stores and Roblox, a confirmation code; Reddit, your click; Shorts and YouTube streams go out private; X tells you what each post costs. What it reads (Gmail, Steam, Search Console) is read-only.

Launch and community #

The In the app: 📅 Calendar view brings together your launch, your posts and your scheduled clips. The Discord bot announces builds and playtests and brings you the /bug reports; the 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Community agent summarizes Discord and Steam and itch reviews. Steam sales, YouTube and your game's website show up in Analytics.

For your tasks and recipes #

With 🔌 Tools for tasks (Connectors › GitHub), Claude can read your issues, your Actions and the calendar, prepare drafts and ask to open an issue, a PR or a Discord announcement, which wait for your approval. Tokens never reach the task: MasterDev makes the call. In the app: Customize › 🧪 Recipes link connectors ("serious bug → GitHub issue", "when a build is published, Discord announcement and X draft"): they start switched off, "▶ Test" shows what they would do, and whatever they publish waits for your yes in Needs you.
