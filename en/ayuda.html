    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need Claude to use MasterDev?

    No. Claude is optional: without it your account, profile, Community, calls, cards, Discover, the Gallery and your projects all work. Only tasks, creating a project with AI and a few features marked “Requires Claude” use it.

    What is a MasterDev server?

    This is where your community lives: friends, messages, servers, shared cards and Discover. Someone hosts it (it can be your PC, in Settings › Network), and you connect with your @username and your MasterDev password.

    I forgot my password

    On the sign-in screen, “Forgot password?” uses your recovery code. If you don't have it either, write to us from “Contact us” with another account, or ask your server admin to reset it.

    Someone is bothering me: what do I do?

    Click their name and choose 🔕 Mute (on your PC only), ⛔ Block or ⚑ Report. Reports reach the moderators with context.

    It says “Reconnecting…” at the top

    The connection to your server dropped. MasterDev retries on its own, waiting a bit longer each time (up to 30 s). Anything you write in the meantime stays “⏳ queued” and is sent once, when it reconnects. “Retry now” tries right away.

    My message got stuck “queued”

    It will be sent automatically as soon as the connection returns, in the same order. If you'd rather not send it, click ✕ on the message.

    One of my images was removed

    Admins review the avatars, banners, cards, icons and emojis that are uploaded. If one doesn't meet the rules, it stops being visible and you get a notice. That same image can't be uploaded again. If you think it's a mistake, write to us.

    How do I get sparks?

    With daily and weekly missions, the card of the day, recycling duplicate cards, playtest rewards and bug hunting. They are never bought with money.

    A pack gave me nothing

    Check Cards › Gifts to open: gifted packs and prizes arrive there. If the connection dropped while opening one, the pack comes back when you reconnect.

    Where are my games?

    In Projects. Each game lives in its folder (projects/<id>/) or in your own if you linked it. ▶ Play opens web projects in the app.

    How do I test my game with other people?

    In the project sheet › 🧪 Playtest: open it to your friends or to everyone, with surveys, recording with permission and markers.

    MasterDev closed or crashed

    When you open it again it will ask whether you want to send the crash report. It has no personal data: your Windows user shows up as <usuario> and no tokens or messages are included. Before sending it you can see exactly what goes out. Change it in Settings › System › Diagnostics.

    I found a bug

    Click 🐞 at the top (or /bug). First check “Known issues” in case it's already on the list. If the admin confirms it, bug hunting earns you sparks.

    It is slow or won’t connect

    Settings › System › Diagnostics measures the server's response and bundles a ZIP for whoever is helping you (no passwords, messages or projects).

    What does the server keep about me?

    Your account, your profile, your messages and what you share. In Settings › Account you can download a ZIP with all your data or delete your account.

    Can I use MasterDev without internet?

    Yes: projects, ideas, notes, music, Gallery and what belongs to your PC work without a network. Social features wait for the connection to return.

    Can't find it?

    In the app you have “💬 Ask MasterDev” in the ?, help in your server's community (with mentors 🎓) and 🐞 Report a problem, with a preview of what gets sent.
