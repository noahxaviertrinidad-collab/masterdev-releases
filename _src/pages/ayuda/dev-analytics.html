Help center · For creators

📈 Analytics

Market data before writing code.

In short: Ask about the video game market and the AI looks it up on the web, with the source for every figure.

Ask about the market #

/analytics and your question (“Roblox games with the highest earnings this week”): Claude researches it on the web and gives you a report with numbered sources. You choose how to view it: bars, lines or pie. There's a spending cap in Settings.
